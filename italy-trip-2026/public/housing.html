Housing — whole-place, weekly, carless

34 nights · Sep 11–Oct 15 Family of 6 · sleeps-6 apartments Belt over center · no car except the Dolomites

Eight bases, one rule: book the whole place, take the weekly rate on the long stays, and never sleep somewhere the trains don't reach. We're carless the entire loop except the Dolomites, so every base has to be walkable to a station, ferry, funicular or metro. "Belt over center" where it helps — a bigger, calmer, cheaper apartment one stop out that's still on the line. The one leg that bends the rule is the Dolomites, where we have a rental car and are staying at an apartment in Laion that no train reaches. All eight are booked and the whole thing landed at $9,403 — $277 a night for six of us, whole-place. Booked is not the same as paid, though: $5,943 of the trip is still scheduled to charge, four of those hits landing in September. The live paid-vs-pending ledger is on Costs.

— Why these eight, in hindsight

The three rules that picked every base

Kept as the record of why each base is what it is — not as instructions. Every bed is booked; nothing here is a decision any more.

The rules we actually applied 01 · Whole place, always. Six people means one apartment sleeping all six in its bedrooms, never a clutch of hotel rooms. Two bedrooms was enough wherever the second held bunks; filtering to three emptied out most cities.

02 · Weekly rate on the long legs. Three of the eight legs are seven nights — Florence, Rome and Venice/Lido — and both Airbnb and Booking drop the nightly price 10–20% at seven. Shoulder season did the rest. That discount is already baked into the paid totals below.

03 · Never sleep where the trains don't reach. Carless for the whole loop except the Dolomites, so every base had to be walkable to a station, ferry, funicular or metro. The one base that bends the rule — Funtnatsch in Laion — bends it on purpose, because that is the leg with the rental car.

The one that bends the rule — on purpose · ✓ booked The Dolomites leg is the one no train reaches, and that's fine because it's the one leg with the rental car (collected at Venice Marco Polo Oct 10, dropped at Malpensa noon Oct 15). Booked: Funtnatsch Apartment Schlern, Laion (Lajen) — $1,029 for 4 nights, conf 5740340654, free cancellation to 10 Sep. 90 m², 2 bedrooms plus a living-room sofa bed for six, one bathroom, free private parking, 9.9/10.

This is the trip's one re-book. The original pick — the Singerhof in St. Valentin, $1,004 — was cancelled by the property on 26 Aug 2026, refund automatic. Re-searching found no whole-place availability for six in Castelrotto, Siusi or Ortisei under $350/night on these dates, so the base moved down to Laion, in the Isarco valley above Ponte Gardena.

The trade is real and worth stating plainly: Alpe di Siusi went from 8 minutes to ~35 and Bolzano from 30 to ~45, while Ortisei came in to ~20 min and Kastelruth to ~15. What Laion buys back is the season — at ~1,100 m it's in the Törggelen belt and fully open in mid-October, where the high resorts are between seasons. Still rural, so the off-street parking filter is settled and the village ZTLs never come up.
