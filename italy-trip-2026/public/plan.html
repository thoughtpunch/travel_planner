— How we move

All rail, one rental

No ferries and no mid-trip flights this year — the whole loop is trains, from the airport express out of Malpensa to the fast Frecciarossa hops between the big cities. The single exception is the Dolomites.

Fast start + coast

Milan → Turin → Chiavari

Frecciarossa Milan→Turin (~1h), then down to the Ligurian coast via Genoa to our Chiavari base (~2h30, change at Genoa). Book the Turin leg, ride regionale to the coast.

The fast spine

Chiavari → Florence → Rome → Venice

Coast to Florence (~2h30), then the high-speed hops: Frecciarossa Florence→Rome (~1h35) and Rome→Venice (~4h, straight through). Book advance fares early — they're a fraction of walk-up.

The one car leg

Lido → Marco Polo → Kastelruth

Alilaguna boat straight from the Lido to VCE (~1h), collect a 7-seater at the airport, drive ~3½h to the Singerhof. No train, no bags through S. Lucia. The mountain valleys need wheels for Kronplatz, Seceda and the passes.

Home

Kastelruth → Malpensa, by car

Drive the whole way — ~355 km, ~4h15 of motorway, lunch at Sirmione on Lake Garda — and drop the car one-way at MXP for the airport-hotel night before the Oct 15 flight. One rental replaces both old rail legs.

— The money

Budget — the shape

The in-Italy ground cost — lodging, food, trains, and activities for 2 adults + 4 boys across 34 nights — is modeled day by day, city by city, on the dedicated Budget page → That's where the low/avg/high bands and the money-saving levers live.

What the ground budget covers

  • Lodging — 3+BR apartments, booked in the walkable belt one stop out of each center at weekly rates.
  • Food — cook 4–5 nights a base, eat the region the rest (this is a food trip).
  • Rail — regional hops plus the advance-fare Frecciarossa legs, with kid discounts.
  • Activities — workshops, timed-entry museums (EU under-18s free at state sites), the Dolomites rental car.
Flights — already paid, kept separate The transatlantic flights are booked and paid: $5,346 (Icelandair both ways via Keflavík, ref AMBVO4 — depart BWI 23:20 Thu Sep 10, land Milan MXP 9:55pm Fri Sep 11; home from MXP 16:20 Thu Oct 15). They sit outside the in-Italy budget model — the Budget page counts only ground costs, so don't double-count. Fare is Economy Light (1 carry-on each, no checked bags — traveling carry-on only by design).
