— How we move

All rail, one rental

No ferries and no mid-trip flights this year — the whole loop is trains, from the airport express out of Malpensa to the three Frecciarossa hops between the big cities. The single exception is the Dolomites.

Regionale + lakes

Milan → Como → Cinque Terre

Short regional hops to Como (~1h), then a longer haul down to the Ligurian coast (~4h, change at Milan/Genoa). Slow trains, cheap tickets, no reservations needed.

The three fast legs

Florence → Rome → Bologna → Venice

The high-speed spine: Frecciarossa Florence→Rome (~1h30), Rome→Bologna (~2h15), Bologna→Venice (~1h30). Book advance fares early — they're a fraction of walk-up.

The one car leg

Venice → Bolzano → Val Gardena

Train up to Bolzano (~3h, change at Verona), then pick up a rental car — the only carless-exception of the trip. The mountain valleys need wheels for Seceda and Alpe di Siusi.

Home

Bolzano → Milan → MXP

Drop the car, train back across the top of Italy to Milan and out to Malpensa for the airport-hotel night before the Oct 15 flight home.

— The money

Budget — the shape

The in-Italy ground cost — lodging, food, trains, and activities for 2 adults + 4 boys across 34 nights — is modeled day by day, city by city, on the dedicated Budget page → That's where the low/avg/high bands and the money-saving levers live.

What the ground budget covers

  • Lodging — 3+BR apartments, booked in the walkable belt one stop out of each center at weekly rates.
  • Food — cook 4–5 nights a base, eat the region the rest (this is a food trip).
  • Rail — regional hops plus the three advance-fare Frecciarossa legs, with kid discounts.
  • Activities — workshops, timed-entry museums (EU under-18s free at state sites), the Dolomites rental car.
Flights — already paid, kept separate The transatlantic flights are booked and paid: $5,346 (Icelandair both ways via Keflavík, ref AMBVO4 — depart BWI 23:20 Thu Sep 10, land Milan MXP 9:55pm Fri Sep 11; home from MXP 16:20 Thu Oct 15). They sit outside the in-Italy budget model — the Budget page counts only ground costs, so don't double-count. Fare is Economy Light (1 carry-on each, no checked bags — traveling carry-on only by design).
