— Weird, wacky & unforgettable

Offbeat & hidden gems

The strange, memorable, off-the-tourist-track finds the boys will actually talk about — vetted, real, and doable from the Lido by vaporetto and foot.

Kei + parents · secret spiral

Scala Contarini del Bovolo

A hidden 15th-century external spiral staircase — 80 stone steps twisting counter-clockwise up a 26m brick-and-arch tower to a rooftop belvedere over the rooftops. Pure Escher, tucked down an alley most tourists never find. Timed 30-min slots, max 20 people; under-12s free.

All ages · book a timed slot ahead

gioiellinascostidivenezia.it
Keir + Jude · boats, from across the water

Squero di San Trovaso

One of Venice's last working gondola boatyards, open since the 17th century, with Alpine-looking wooden huts on the water. You can't go in, but the craftsmen don't mind you watching them build and repair gondolas from the bank across the narrow Rio San Trovaso — boatbuilding engineering, live and free.

All ages · free · Dorsoduro / Zattere

aviewoncities.com
Keir + Rhys · stand-up gondola

The €2 traghetto crossing

The locals' hack: a two-oared traghetto gondola ferries you straight across the Grand Canal for about €2, and tradition says you stand the whole way. A one-minute real gondola ride for pocket change instead of €90 — a small thrill and a genuine Venetian everyday ritual.

All ages · ~€2 cash · 7 crossing points

veneziaunica.it
Kei + parents · hands-on craft

Ca' Macana mask workshop

The Dorsoduro atelier that made the masks for Kubrick's Eyes Wide Shut runs hands-on courses where you build and decorate your own Venetian Carnival mask using the traditional papier-mâché techniques. Weird, beautiful, and everyone goes home with the thing they made.

Age 6+ · book the decorating course ahead

camacana.com
Parents + Keir · lagoon lore

Torcello's Devil's Bridge & Attila's throne

Venice's near-empty birthplace island hides a railless stone "Devil's Bridge" said to have been built in a single night by the devil to win a bet, and a marble "throne of Attila" — sit in it and legend says you'll return to Torcello. Ancient, eerie, and gloriously strange. Reachable by vaporetto via Burano.

All ages · vaporetto 12 + 9 · quiet all-day outing

isoladiburano.it
Grey + parents · leaning tower + live craft

Burano's crooked campanile & Lace Museum

Burano has its own leaning bell tower — the campanile storto visibly tilting in the marshy soil — plus the Lace Museum on Galuppi Square where elderly Buranese women still demonstrate needle-lace by hand, a craft the island has guarded since the 1500s. Candy-colored houses all around.

All ages · vaporetto 12 · combine with Torcello

museomerletto.visitmuve.it
Rhys + parents · edgy / dark history

The plague islands by boat

Out in the lagoon lie Venice's quarantine islands — the lazzaretti — and infamous Poveglia, sealed to the public, where plague dead were burned and, later, an asylum stood. You can't land on Poveglia, but private "ghost island" boat tours circle the ruins and land at Lazzaretto Vecchio for the full grim story. Peak Voynich energy.

Older kids + adults · private boat tour, book ahead

getyourguide.com
Everyone · only-in-Venice weather

Acqua alta & the raised walkways

Early October can bring an acqua alta tide that floods St Mark's — and the city's answer is wonderfully odd: instant elevated walkways (passerelle) snapped together on trestles so life goes on above the water. Check the official tide forecast, pack boots, and watch a city calmly walk on stilts.

All ages · free · check the tide app before you go

comune.venezia.it
