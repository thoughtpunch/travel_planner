Florence — the 18th & the maker's week

Sept 18 – 25 · 7 nights

We land in Florence the day Rhys becomes an adult, and stay a full week — the longest of the trip. It's the perfect base for his real interests: multi-hour artisan courses and a birthday worth remembering.

Sept 18 · the birthday

A Florentine 18th — pasta splurge & first legal aperitivo

Now legal across Europe · the day we arrive

Make it the day's centerpiece: sunset over the whole Renaissance skyline from Piazzale Michelangelo, his first proper aperitivo, then a splurge, pasta-focused birthday dinner to mark it — and his first legal glass of Chianti. The full plan and the dinner reservation live on the celebrations page.

celebrations · Rhys's 18th
Santa Croce · a real trade

Florentine leather workshop — Scuola del Cuoio

Hands-on · English · half-day & multi-session options

The historic leather school behind Santa Croce runs hands-on workshops where you cut, stitch and gild your own piece — a genuine Florentine craft, finished in a day, that he takes home. Exactly the kind of grown-up course he likes.

scuoladelcuoio.com
Oltrarno · marbled paper & binding

Bookbinding & paper-marbling — Il Torchio

Small-group workshop · English · book by email

In an Oltrarno artisan bottega, learn hand bookbinding and Florentine marbled paper — swirl the pigments, bind your own notebook. Quiet, absorbing, and very him.

legatoriailtorchio.com
City center · keep your ring

Gemstone setting — Le Arti Orafe

1-day craft experience · English · confirm 18-yo OK by email

Set a real gemstone onto a silver ring and take it home — Florence's goldsmithing school runs a 1-day craft experience in English. A genuine skill, finished in a day.

artiorafe.it · craft experience
Chianti · day trip · first legal wine country

A Tuscan wine-country day

Now old enough to taste · Greve / Chianti Classico · English tours

A slow day out in the Chianti hills — a farm lunch, a cellar, the vineyards between Florence and Siena. The kind of afternoon that only opens up now that he's 18.

chianticlassico.com
Scarperia · Mugello · blades

Knife-forging day (Scarperia)

18 — no problem · forge your own knife · ~€100 · ~45 min from Florence

The historic Tuscan knife town runs forge-your-own-blade days — an easy day trip from the Florence base and perfect for the eldest. ⚠ Published dates skew spring — email for a Sept session; Italian-leaning, use a translator.

sos2012.it · knife forging
