— Friday, September 18 · Florence

Rhys turns eighteen

The 18th lands on the very first evening in Florence — arrival day. That's a gift: you roll off the train into the most beautiful city on the route, and the birthday becomes the welcome. Keep the day loose (travel + settle in), then make the evening count. He's legally an adult in Italy now — lean into it.

🌇
The shape of the evening: sunset at Piazzale Michelangelo (the whole city + Duomo glowing gold, gelato in hand) → walk down through the Oltrarno → the splurge pasta dinner → his first legal drink where the drink was invented.
Sep 18 · PM

The splurge pasta dinner — three ways to go

The celebration pick

Ristorante La Giostra

A theatrical, fairy-lit Florentine institution that lives for a birthday — welcome prosecco, a fuss made, and the famous pear-and-pecorino ravioli plus hand-cut pastas and Florentine steak. Festive enough for an 18th, warm enough for the little brothers. Book a table for six well ahead and tell them it's Rhys's 18th.

ristorantelagiostra.com
The pasta purist

Osteria delle Tre Panche

A pilgrimage for pasta lovers — the truffle tagliolini is legendary. Smaller and more serious; the move if the birthday wish is "the best plate of pasta in Florence" over party atmosphere. Reserve early; it's tiny and adored.

trepanche.it
The refined splurge

Il Santo Bevitore

Oltrarno, cand-lit, grown-up but relaxed — beautiful fresh pastas and a proper wine list, a notch more polished for a milestone dinner. Good if you want "special-occasion restaurant" without stiffness. Book the back room for six.

ilsantobevitore.com
His first legal drink — and it's a Florence native The Negroni was invented in Florence in 1919 (Count Camillo Negroni asked Caffè Casoni to stiffen his Americano). So Rhys's first legal cocktail should be a Negroni in its hometown — do it as a toast, at a proper bar like Caffè Rivoire on Piazza della Signoria or a classic aperitivo spot before dinner. A perfect, meaningful "you're 18" moment.

Make it an 18th, not just a dinner — during the week

A grown-up skill

A Florentine artisan course

Give him a real hands-on day the way he likes — leatherwork at the Scuola del Cuoio (make a wallet/belt), or paper-marbling / bookbinding. A made-thing to carry home from the city where he turned 18.

scuoladelcuoio.com
A taste of independence

Some solo rope

Eighteen wants a little autonomy — an afternoon to roam Florence on his own, or a Vespa-tour / Chianti wine day (now that he can taste). Small freedoms that mark the birthday as a threshold, not just a party.

Don't forget

Cake + candles

Order a torta from a Florentine pasticceria (or ask the restaurant) so there's a candle to blow out, and a scoop of the good gelato. Eighteen still deserves a cake with his name on it.

— Friday, October 9 · the Dolomites

Dan & Kei's anniversary

The anniversary is the arrival day in the Dolomites — you take the train up, collect the car in Bolzano, and drive into the mountains as the light goes. The plan is simple and worth booking properly: a beautiful mountain-hotel dinner, just the two of you, with the boys fed and happy back at the chalet.

🍷
The move: settle the boys with a pizza-and-movie night at the chalet (Rhys, 18, holds the fort), then Dan & Kei to a gourmet Ladin dinner in Val Gardena. A quiet, grown-up evening in the Alps to mark the day.
Oct 9 · PM
The special dinner

Anna Stuben · Ortisei

A jewel-box Michelin dining room in wood-panelled Tyrolean stube style at the Gardena Grödnerhof — refined Alpine-Ladin cooking, candlelight, a serious wine list. The classic "beautiful mountain-hotel dinner" for an anniversary. Confirm it's open Oct 9 (autumn season dates vary) and reserve.

gardena.it · Anna Stuben
Alternative · Selva

Alpenroyal Gourmet

Another Michelin table a few minutes up the valley in Selva, in a grand alpine hotel — same idea, superb tasting menus and a romantic room. A strong plan B if Anna Stuben is on its seasonal break that week.

alpenroyal.com
The romantic add-on

A sunset up top

If a lift is still running that evening, ride up for the alpenglow on the Sassolungo before dinner — or catch it from a terrace with a glass of Alto Adige white. Ten minutes that make the day feel like the occasion it is.

Dolomites page →
Book early — and check the season Early-to-mid October is the shoulder between summer and ski in South Tyrol; some gourmet restaurants and hotels take an autumn Ruhepause closure. Reserve the anniversary dinner as soon as the base is set, and confirm the exact October opening dates when you book the Dolomites chalet.
— Monday, October 12 · the Dolomites

Grey turns twelve

A birthday in the middle of the most dramatic mountains in Europe. The plan writes itself: a cable-car up to a jaw-dropping ridge, an alpine lunch at a mountain hut, and a birthday dessert with a candle in it — then pizza back down in the valley for the boy who loves to make it.

🚡
The day: Ortisei → Seceda cable car to the knife-edge ridge → walk to a rifugio for lunch → Kaiserschmarrn or apple strudel with a candle → toboggan/playground on the way down → pizza dinner in Ortisei.
Oct 12 · all day
The scenic experience

Seceda cable car + ridge

The two-stage lift from Ortisei rises to the Seceda ridgeline — the tilted-slab peaks that look computer-generated. An easy stroll along the top delivers the view of the trip. If Seceda's lift is on autumn break, swap in the Alpe di Siusi / Mont Sëuc cable car for Europe's largest alpine meadow.

valgardena.it · lifts
Alpine lunch + dessert

Lunch at a rifugio

A mountain hut lunch — Rifugio Firenze / Baita Sofie / Malga — hearty and kid-friendly (canederli, goulash, and the all-important Kaiserschmarrn or apfelstrudel for the birthday candle). Grey's the family pizza-and-dessert maker, so a proper alpine sweet with a candle is his moment.

huts on the Dolomites page →
Kid wins

Toboggan + pizza night

Work in a mountain toboggan/alpine coaster or a playground on the way down (all four boys), then a pizza dinner in Ortisei to close the birthday. Grab a small cake or let him pick the gelato.

Grey's activities →
Confirm the lift calendar for Oct 12 Val Gardena's summer lifts typically run to mid-October, then close for the between-seasons break — Oct 12 is close to the edge. Check the Seceda and Alpe di Siusi operating dates before locking the day, and keep the Ötzi museum in Bolzano + the Renon earth pyramids as the all-weather, all-season backup birthday plan.
