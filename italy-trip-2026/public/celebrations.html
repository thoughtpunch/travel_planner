— Friday, September 18 · the Ligurian Coast

Rhys turns eighteen

The 18th lands on the last night on the coast — the close of four days of sea and rest before the trip turns inland to Florence. That's the setting: a seafood dinner right over the Mediterranean, the sun going down on the water, and a birthday that feels like a proper send-off from the Ligurian shore. He's legally an adult in Italy now — lean into it.

🌅
The shape of the evening: a last swim or seafront stroll as the light drops → the seafood dinner over the water (fritto misto, the day's catch, Ligurian white) → his first legal drink, a toast to the sea. Simple, and hard to beat.
Sep 18 · PM

The seafood dinner over the sea — three ways to go

The easy home pick

A seafront table in Chiavari

Stay in our base town: a proper trattoria di mare on the Chiavari waterfront — fritto misto, catch of the day, Ligurian pasta, and no drive home after. Festive enough for an 18th, easy with all four boys. Book a table for six and tell them it's Rhys's birthday.

The postcard splurge

Camogli or Sestri Levante

Ride one stop to a table right over the water — Camogli's pastel harbour or the Baia del Silenzio at Sestri Levante. Grilled fish and a sunset on the bay for the milestone. A short train hop back to base afterwards.

The glam option

Santa Margherita / Portofino

If you want the full Riviera postcard, a dinner over the harbour at Santa Margherita or Portofino — dressier, pricier, unforgettable. The move if the birthday wish is "make it feel like a scene." Reserve well ahead.

— Friday, October 9 · Venice

Dan & Kei's anniversary

The anniversary falls near the end of the Venice week — the perfect night to slip away onto the water. The plan is simple and worth booking properly: a dinner on the canals, just the two of you, with the boys fed and happy back at the Lido apartment.

🚤
The move: settle the boys with a pizza-and-movie night at the Lido place (Rhys, 18, holds the fort), then Dan & Kei by vaporetto into Venice for a canalside dinner and a slow wander over the bridges after dark. A quiet, grown-up evening on the water to mark the day.
Oct 9 · PM
The special dinner

A table on the canal

Book a romantic waterside table in Venice — a quiet rio in Cannaregio or Dorsoduro, candlelight, the water lapping just past the railing, and a proper seafood-and-wine dinner. The classic "dinner on the canals" for an anniversary. Reserve a canalside table for two well ahead.

The local move

A cicchetti crawl

Less formal, very Venetian — a bàcaro crawl through Cannaregio, hopping tiny bars for cicchetti and ombre of wine along the Fondamenta della Misericordia. Grown-up, delicious, and pure Venice. Wander canal to canal, no reservation needed.

The romantic add-on

The water home

Close the night on the water: a late vaporetto down the Grand Canal with the palazzi lit up, or splash out on a short gondola through the quiet back canals. Ten minutes that make the day feel like the occasion it is.

Book early — and mind the tides The best canalside tables in Venice are small and sell out, so reserve the anniversary dinner as soon as the week is set. Early October can bring an evening acqua alta high tide — keep an eye on the forecast and have a backstreet trattoria in mind as a dry-feet backup.
— Monday, October 12 · the Dolomites

Grey turns twelve

A birthday in the middle of the most dramatic mountains in Europe. The plan writes itself: a cable-car up to a jaw-dropping ridge, an alpine lunch at a mountain hut, and a birthday dessert with a candle in it — then pizza back down in the valley for the boy who loves to make it.

🚡
The day: drive ~20 min from Laion to Ortisei → Seceda cable car to the knife-edge ridge → rifugio lunch with Kaiserschmarrn or apple strudel and a candle → toboggan/playground on the way down → back by six for the birthday dinner at the Tschötscherhof, ~20 min from the house.
Oct 12 · all day

The scenic experience

Seceda cable car + ridge

The two-stage lift from Ortisei rises to the Seceda ridgeline — the tilted-slab peaks that look computer-generated. An easy stroll along the top delivers the view of the trip. If Seceda's lift is on autumn break, swap in the Alpe di Siusi / Mont Sëuc cable car for Europe's largest alpine meadow.

Alpine lunch + dessert

Lunch at a rifugio

A mountain hut lunch — Rifugio Firenze / Baita Sofie / Malga — hearty and kid-friendly (canederli, goulash, and the all-important Kaiserschmarrn or apfelstrudel for the birthday candle). Grey's the family pizza-and-dessert maker, so a proper alpine sweet with a candle is his moment.

★ The birthday dinner — Tschötscherhof, and it's confirmed open Grey wants meaty, cheesy, comforting and enormous — not a tasting menu. The old plan here was a "gourmet Ladin dinner" at a Val Gardena mountain hotel; that's precisely the little-plates-of-things he doesn't want, so it's been dropped.

The pick: Gasthof Tschötscherhof, St. Oswald, Kastelruth — about 10 minutes from the house. A 500-year-old family-run farm inn down a lane away from any road noise, cooking Tyrolean farm food largely from its own land: speck, canederli dumplings, roasts, mountain cheese, strudel. Low beams, wood stove, rated 9.1. It's also a listed Törggelen house, and Oct 12 sits in the middle of Törggelen season — so the autumn feast menu is the whole reason to go then rather than any other week.

Confirmed open 21 Jun – 2 Nov 2026, daily. The catch worth knowing: the warm kitchen runs 12:00–14:00 and 18:00–20:30 only. That is an early dinner by Italian standards — book a 18:30 table and plan the mountain day to be back down by six.

If Grey wants fondue or raclette instead: Bistrot B24 in Selva (~35 min) keeps an in-house fromagerie — a small private room with a heating element in every table and its own ventilation — doing cheese fondue and classic raclette, and it's a pizzeria too, so it covers both of his favourite things at one table. But phone +39 0471 774444 first: we could not confirm its autumn dates, evenings only, and Val Gardena half-shuts between the summer and ski seasons.

Pure-pizza fallback, five minutes away: Pizzeria Bachler in Kastelruth (wood-fired, mountain-view terrace, best regarded in the village) or Zur Alten Schmiede in the centre. No booking drama, works on any night.
The Oct 12 lift calendar is confirmed — go ahead and lock the day Checked with the operators on 21 Aug 2026. The old worry — "Val Gardena's lifts close mid-October" — conflated the Gardena Card (which does end 11 Oct) with the lifts themselves. Seceda runs to 2 Nov, Mont Sëuc to 2 Nov, the Siusi cableway to 1 Nov, Kronplatz to 8 Nov. Neither Oct 11 nor Oct 12 is close to the edge.

One genuine casualty: the Fermeda chairlift closed 20 Sep, so the ridge walk from the Seceda top station is on foot — fine for this crowd, but don't plan on the shortcut. Keep Ötzi and the Renon earth pyramids as the wet-weather backup, which is now the only thing they're backing up.

Bonus: the Alpe di Siusi road is shut to cars 09:00–17:00 but reopens after 17:00 — so a sunset birthday dinner up at Compatsch (~1,850 m, sunset ~18:30) is on the table if Grey would rather eat on a mountain than in the village.

One thing still to check: the lifts are confirmed, but mountain huts are a separate calendar and many rifugi close in mid-October regardless of whether the lift below them is running. Ring the specific hut before building the birthday lunch around it — or treat the Compatsch huts, which are easier to reach and later-closing, as the fallback.
