The Cinque Terre Card

The one thing to buy before anything else. Because we're carless, the local train is our legs.

Cinque Terre Treno MS Card · Trenitalia + Parco Nazionale One card covers unlimited local trains between Levanto and La Spezia (stopping at all five villages), plus access to the coastal walking trails and station wifi. The local train hops 5–20 minutes village to village — Levanto → Monterosso → Vernazza → Corniglia → Manarola → Riomaggiore → La Spezia — so you can bounce down the coast all day. Buy it at any station machine/ticket office or online at card.parconazionale5terre.it (info: parconazionale5terre.it). A daily card is cheaper than paying per hop, and EU under-18s get reduced/free rates where applicable — check current family pricing at the counter.
— The shortlist

Cinque Terre Top 10

Ranked for a family that wants to swim, walk a bit, ride boats, and eat well — not tick off all five villages in a forced march.

  1. 1
    Manarola — the classic viewpoint
    Punta Bonfiglio · golden hour

    The postcard: houses stacked over a rocky swimming cove. Walk out to the Nessun Dorma terrace bar for bruschetta and the famous view; the little harbor cove is a fine dip.

    All ages · parconazionale5terre.it
  2. 2
    Vernazza — harbor + Doria castle
    prettiest port · climb the Belforte tower

    The most beautiful of the five: a natural harbor ringed by tall houses, with the medieval Doria castle (Castello Doria) above for a small climb and a big view. Gelato on the piazza, feet in the water.

    All ages · parconazionale5terre.it
  3. 3
    Monterosso al Mare — the real beach
    Fegina beach · loungers + free stretches

    The only village with a proper sandy beach — the swim-and-sandcastle day for Keir and Grey. Rent an umbrella on Fegina or find a free patch, then walk into the old town for lunch.

    All ages · beach day
  4. 4
    Sentiero Azzurro — the Blue Trail hikes
    Monterosso–Vernazza is the scenic one · check openings

    The famous coastal footpath. Some segments close after rain, so check what's open the morning of. Monterosso–Vernazza is the classic scenic leg; the low Via dell'Amore (Manarola–Riomaggiore) recently reopened and may need a timed booking.

    Older kids + adults · trail status · parconazionale5terre.it
  5. 5
    Riomaggiore at sunset
    the marina slipway · aperitivo hour

    The southernmost village catches the evening light down its stacked lane to the sea. Sit on the marina rocks with a spritz and focaccia while the boats glow. Easy last train back to Levanto/La Spezia.

    All ages · free
  6. 6
    Village-hopping by ferry
    Consorzio Marittimo Turistico 5 Terre

    See the coast the way it was meant to be seen — from the water. The ferry links Levanto, Monterosso, Vernazza, Manarola and Riomaggiore (not Corniglia, which is up a cliff). A day pass is the scenic alternative to the tunnels of the train.

    All ages · navigazionegolfodeipoeti.it
  7. 7
    Focaccia, farinata & trofie al pesto
    every village · takeaway + trattoria

    Ligurian street food is the point. Warm focaccia by the slice, farinata (chickpea flatbread from the wood oven), and trofie al pesto — this is pesto's home turf. A rolling tasting, village to village.

    Family · takeaway
  8. 8
    Manarola vineyards & sciacchetrà
    the terraced cliffs above town

    The dry-stone terraces holding these villages up are vineyards. Walk the vine paths above Manarola and taste the rare sweet sciacchetrà the region makes from dried grapes — a grown-up sip with a very old story.

    Adults · parconazionale5terre.it
  9. 9
    A boat or swim day
    coves, jump rocks, a small charter

    Blue-flag water and rocky coves everywhere. Swim the Manarola cove, jump off the rocks at Vernazza, or split a small private boat for a half-day to reach swim spots the crowds can't. The one big activity Keir will remember.

    All ages · swim
  10. 10
    Levanto — our relaxed base
    beach town · gateway to the tunnels

    Just north of the park: a real town with a broad beach, easy parking-free life, and the start of the flat old-railway bike/walk path through lit tunnels to Bonassola and Framura. Quieter to sleep in, one stop from Monterosso.

    All ages · comune.levanto.sp.it

Pesto-making class

Mortar, pestle, Ligurian basil, pine nuts, Parmigiano and pecorino — learn to make the real thing by hand where it was invented, then eat it over trofie. Hands-on and delicious.

The region's cult sweet wine, pressed from grapes dried on the terraces. A tiny production, a big flavor — track down a tasting in Manarola or Riomaggiore for a proper adult moment.

The gorgeous village the Cinque Terre forgets — pastel houses, the cliff-edge church of San Pietro, and Byron's Grotto below, where the poet swam. An easy boat day from La Spezia.

— Weird, wacky & unforgettable

Offbeat & hidden gems

The strange, memorable, off-the-tourist-track finds the boys will actually talk about — vetted, real, and doable carless by train, boat and foot from Levanto.

Jude & the parents · ruin-weird

Il Gigante, the bombed sea-god

A 46-foot concrete Neptune from 1910, half-melted into the Monterosso cliff. WWII bombing tore off his arms, trident and the giant scallop shell he once held; the sea finished the job in the '66 storms. Restored in 2022, he now looms over Fegina beach like a wrecked Escher colossus — pure Voynich strangeness, and a five-minute walk from the station.

All ages · free, on the Fegina seafront in Monterosso

lecinqueterre.org
Rhys · pirate-tower sundowner

Torre Aurora, the pirate watchtower

A stone tower built around the year 1000 to spot Saracen pirate raids, jutting out on the headland between Monterosso's two halves. Since 2017 it's a tiny cliff-edge lounge with 360° sea terraces — the spot to put a proper glass of Cinque Terre white in front of an 18-year-old Rhys at sunset while everyone else eats gelato below.

18+ for the bar · walk-up terrace, book a table for sunset

torreauroracinqueterre.it
Jude · engineering oddity

The vineyard monorail trains

There's no road up these terraces, so growers ride tiny one-person cog-rail monorails — 50-odd of them — bolted to stilts up near-vertical vineyards to haul grapes down. First built at Volastra in 1980, the first in Italy. Mid-September is harvest, so we'll actually see them running; some Volastra cellars fold a monorail ride into a tasting.

Great for Jude · Volastra above Manarola, short train + walk; book a cellar visit

ilvigneto5terre.com
Everyone · cliff-carved & reborn

Via dell'Amore, finally reopened

The blasted-into-the-cliff lovers' walk between Riomaggiore and Manarola was shut for 12 years after a rockfall. It reopened in 2024 and for 2026 is folded into the standard Cinque Terre Card — but you still book a timed 30-minute entry slot. A short, jaw-dropping ledge over the sea; grab a slot the morning you want it.

All ages · timed ticket required, reserve at viadellamore.info

viadellamore.info
Grey · weird-good food

Monterosso's salt-cured anchovies

The "bread of the sea" — anchovies caught off Monterosso and packed radially in salt in chestnut barrels, a Slow Food Presidium. Far from a slimy tin: fresh, buttery, eaten on warm focaccia. There's even a Sagra delle Acciughe Salate anchovy festival in Monterosso in September, right in our window.

Grey will either love it or make a face · Monterosso trattorias & the Sept festival

lecinqueterre.org
Keir & Rhys · secret swim

Guvano & hidden coves by kayak

Guvano, the near-legendary beach below Corniglia, has no road and barely a path — the only real way in is by sea. Rent a kayak or grab a water taxi out of Vernazza or Monterosso and paddle to inlets like Canneto and Fossola, or the hidden grotto tucked around the rock from Vernazza's beach. Swim where the day-trippers can't reach.

Strong swimmers · kayak/water-taxi from the villages, weather-dependent

cinqueterredalmare.com
Jude & the parents · mystic climb

The Way of the Sanctuaries

Above each village, a hilltop shrine — Soviore over Monterosso is Liguria's oldest, tied to a legend of a Pietà buried in AD 740 and dug up by a scent of roses. Steep dry-stone trails (no cars ever touched them) link the sanctuaries with quieter, higher, weirder views than the coast path. A mystic backbone to the whole coast.

Fit hikers · park trails from each village, free; check trail status first

parconazionale5terre.it
Rhys & Keir · real cliffs

Sea-cliff climbing at Muzzerone

Just past Portovenere, the Muzzerone crag drops straight into the Gulf of Poets — one of Italy's classic sea-cliff climbing spots, run with certified guides for total beginners up. Rope up over open water, then look back at the whole coast. The edgy, earn-it day for the older two.

Guided, ~10+ with a guide · boat/bus to Portovenere, book a half-day
