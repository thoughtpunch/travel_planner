Four nights on the Riviera, and this leg is sea + rest, done by train — not a five-village death-march. We base in Chiavari, a real working town with its own beach, arcaded old streets and a main-line station dead-center on the Tigullio gulf: ~25 min west to Camogli, ~10 min to Sestri Levante, ~40+ min east to the Cinque Terre. — Get this first

Base Chiavari + the Cinque Terre Card

Two decisions make this leg easy. First, we sleep in Chiavari — cheaper and more real than the resort villages, and dead-center on the coastal rail line. Second, when we go to the Cinque Terre, one card is the whole transport puzzle solved.

Why Chiavari, and how the card works Chiavari sits on the main Genoa–La Spezia line, so the whole coast is a train ride away and we never touch a car: Camogli/San Fruttuoso boats to the west, the Cinque Terre to the east, Sestri Levante ten minutes off. For the five villages, buy the Cinque Terre Treno MS Card — unlimited local trains Levanto ↔ La Spezia stopping at all five villages, plus trail access and station wifi; the little train hops village to village in 5–20 minutes. Ride a regionale from Chiavari to Monterosso/Levanto, then hop all day on the card. Buy at any station machine or online at card.parconazionale5terre.it (info: parconazionale5terre.it). Under-18s get reduced/free rates where applicable — check family pricing at the counter.
— The shortlist

The coast Top 10

Ranked for a family that wants to swim, ride boats, walk a bit and eat well — with rest built in, not five villages force-marched in a day. The one boat-only adventure outranks "completing" the Cinque Terre.

  1. 1
    Camogli → San Fruttuoso by boat
    the "wow" · weather-dependent day

    A tiny abbey in a cove with no road in — you reach it only by boat or a cliff hike. From storybook Camogli, take the little ferry around the Portofino headland to San Fruttuoso: a medieval abbey right on the beach, a swim off the sand, and the submerged Christ of the Abyss statue offshore. Save it for a calm-sea day and it's the thing everyone remembers.

    All ages · sea permitting · visitgenoa.it
  2. 2
    Genoa on arrival — Galata + a real submarine
    Turin→coast, Tue Sep 15 · midday stop

    Break the journey in Genoa: the Galata Museo del Mare is the Mediterranean's biggest maritime museum, and moored right outside is the Nazario Sauro S-518 — a decommissioned Italian navy submarine you walk through, bunk to torpedo room, afloat in the water. Peak boy-catnip, then continue to Chiavari.

    All ages · galatamuseodelmare.it
  3. 3
    Cinque Terre by train — the pastel three
    Cinque Terre Card · Vernazza · Manarola · Monterosso

    Do the five villages the sane way: pick three and hop them on the card. Vernazza for the postcard harbor and Doria castle, Manarola for the classic viewpoint and its swimming cove, Monterosso for the only proper sandy beach. One ferry leg between them if the seas cooperate.

    All ages · parconazionale5terre.it
  4. 4
    A rest & beach day on the Tigullio
    Chiavari's own waterfront · zero trains

    One day we go nowhere. Chiavari has a broad beach and a long seafront promenade, plus arcaded medieval streets (the caruggi) to drift with a gelato. Decompress between the boat days and the village hops — the low-key day a four-night leg is built around.

    All ages · beach day · comune.chiavari.ge.it
  5. 5
    Baia del Silenzio, Sestri Levante
    10 min east by train · the "bay of silence"

    A near-perfect crescent of pastel houses curling around a calm, shallow bay — one of the prettiest little beaches in Italy, and the gentlest swim on this stretch of coast. Ten minutes from base, ideal for Keir and Grey on a lower-key afternoon.

    All ages · calm swim · sestri-levante.net
  6. 6
    Camogli + the Portofino headland
    ~25 min west · fishing town, no glitz

    Before or after the San Fruttuoso boat, wander Camogli itself — a stack of tall, trompe-l'œil painted houses over a working fishing harbor, quieter and more real than famous Portofino next door. Swim off the pebble beach, eat fried anchovies, watch the boats.

    All ages · visitgenoa.it
  7. 7
    Focaccia di Recco & the pesto homeland
    Recco, Camogli, every village

    This coast is the food. Recco (right on our line) invented focaccia di Recco — two paper-thin sheets of dough oozing molten stracchino cheese, an IGP thing you eat hot. Add classic Ligurian focaccia, farinata, and trofie al pesto in pesto's actual birthplace. A rolling, delicious mission.

    Family · takeaway + trattoria
  8. 8
    Village-hopping by ferry
    Golfo dei Poeti / Tigullio boats

    See the coast the way it's meant to be seen — from the water. Ferries link the Cinque Terre villages (all but cliff-top Corniglia) and, up our way, the Tigullio boats connect Chiavari, Portofino and Camogli. Scenic, breezy, and a break from the tunnels of the train.

    All ages · seasonal timetable · navigazionegolfodeipoeti.it
  9. 9
    A stretch of the Blue Trail
    Monterosso–Vernazza is the scenic one

    Not the whole thing — just one good leg. Monterosso–Vernazza is the classic scenic hike; segments close after rain, so check what's open the morning of. The low Via dell'Amore (Manarola–Riomaggiore) reopened and needs a timed slot. Walk one leg, train the rest.

    Older kids + adults · trail status
  10. 10
    A swim / small-boat day
    coves, jump rocks, a half-day charter

    Blue-flag water and rocky coves all along here. Swim the Manarola cove, jump off the rocks at Vernazza, or split a small private boat for a half-day to reach swim spots the crowds can't — the one big on-the-water thing Keir will talk about all year.

    All ages · swim · weather-dependent
— More to do

Deeper cuts

Grown-ups love

Pesto-making class

Mortar, pestle, Ligurian basil, pine nuts, Parmigiano and pecorino — learn to make the real thing by hand where it was invented, then eat it over trofie. Hands-on and delicious, easy to book out of Genoa or the Tigullio towns.

visitgenoa.it

Portovenere & the Gulf of Poets

The gorgeous village the Cinque Terre forgets — pastel houses, the cliff-edge church of San Pietro, and Byron's Grotto below, where the poet swam. An easy boat day from La Spezia, and the gateway to the Muzzerone sea-cliffs.

navigazionegolfodeipoeti.it

Sciacchetrà & a terrace aperitivo

The Cinque Terre's cult sweet wine, pressed from grapes dried on the terraces — a tiny production, a big flavor. Track down a tasting in Manarola or Riomaggiore, or just take a spritz to a cliff terrace at golden hour.

parconazionale5terre.it

Beach day at Monterosso, Sestri or Chiavari

The three spots with real sand and space to dig, swim and lounge — Monterosso's Fegina, Sestri's Baia del Silenzio, or our own Chiavari waterfront. Umbrellas for the grown-ups, water for the boys, the decompression day between trains.

sestri-levante.net

Ferry + gelato-and-focaccia crawl

Let the boat do the walking: hop two or three villages by ferry, with a focaccia slice and a gelato in each. High reward, low effort — the pace Keir sets, and the antidote to a hot day of stairs.

navigazionegolfodeipoeti.it

Christ of the Abyss snorkel, San Fruttuoso

A bronze Christ stands on the seabed off San Fruttuoso, arms raised toward the surface. Strong swimmers can snorkel out to see him below the waves, or peer down from a glass-bottom boat — an eerie, unforgettable add-on to the abbey-cove day.

— Weird, wacky & unforgettable

Offbeat & hidden gems

The strange, memorable, off-the-tourist-track finds the boys will actually talk about — vetted, real, and doable carless by train, boat and foot from Chiavari.

Jude & the parents · ruin-weird

Il Gigante, the bombed sea-god

A 46-foot concrete Neptune from 1910, half-melted into the Monterosso cliff. WWII bombing tore off his arms, trident and the giant scallop shell he once held; the sea finished the job in the '66 storms. Restored in 2022, he now looms over Fegina beach like a wrecked Escher colossus — pure Voynich strangeness, five minutes from the Monterosso station.

All ages · free, on the Fegina seafront in Monterosso

lecinqueterre.org
Rhys · pirate-tower sundowner

Torre Aurora, the pirate watchtower

A stone tower built around the year 1000 to spot Saracen pirate raids, jutting out on the headland between Monterosso's two halves. Since 2017 it's a tiny cliff-edge lounge with 360° sea terraces — the spot to put a proper glass of Cinque Terre white in front of newly-18 Rhys at sunset while everyone else eats gelato below.

18+ for the bar · walk-up terrace, book a table for sunset

torreauroracinqueterre.it
Jude · engineering oddity

The vineyard monorail trains

There's no road up these terraces, so growers ride tiny one-person cog-rail monorails — 50-odd of them — bolted to stilts up near-vertical vineyards to haul grapes down. First built at Volastra in 1980, the first in Italy. Mid-September is harvest, so we'll actually see them running; some Volastra cellars fold a monorail ride into a tasting.

Great for Jude · Volastra above Manarola, short train + walk; book a cellar visit

ilvigneto5terre.com
Everyone · cliff-carved & reborn

Via dell'Amore, finally reopened

The blasted-into-the-cliff lovers' walk between Riomaggiore and Manarola was shut for 12 years after a rockfall. It reopened in 2024 and for 2026 is folded into the standard Cinque Terre Card — but you still book a timed 30-minute entry slot. A short, jaw-dropping ledge over the sea; grab a slot the morning you want it.

All ages · timed ticket required, reserve at viadellamore.info

viadellamore.info
Grey · weird-good food

Monterosso's salt-cured anchovies

The "bread of the sea" — anchovies caught off Monterosso and packed radially in salt in chestnut barrels, a Slow Food Presidium. Far from a slimy tin: fresh, buttery, eaten on warm focaccia. There's even a Sagra delle Acciughe Salate anchovy festival in Monterosso in September, right in our window.

Grey will either love it or make a face · Monterosso trattorias & the Sept festival

lecinqueterre.org
Keir & Rhys · secret swim

Guvano & hidden coves by kayak

Guvano, the near-legendary beach below Corniglia, has no road and barely a path — the only real way in is by sea. Rent a kayak or grab a water taxi out of Vernazza or Monterosso and paddle to inlets like Canneto and Fossola, or the hidden grotto tucked around the rock from Vernazza's beach. Swim where the day-trippers can't reach.

Strong swimmers · kayak/water-taxi from the villages, weather-dependent

cinqueterredalmare.com
Rhys & Keir · real cliffs

Sea-cliff climbing at Muzzerone

Just past Portovenere, the Muzzerone crag drops straight into the Gulf of Poets — one of Italy's classic sea-cliff climbing spots, run with certified guides for total beginners up. Rope up over open water, then look back at the whole coast. The edgy, earn-it day for the older two.

Guided, ~10+ with a guide · boat/bus to Portovenere, book a half-day

arbaspaa.com
Jude & the parents · mystic climb

The Way of the Sanctuaries

Above each Cinque Terre village, a hilltop shrine — Soviore over Monterosso is Liguria's oldest, tied to a legend of a Pietà buried in AD 740 and dug up by a scent of roses. Steep dry-stone trails (no cars ever touched them) link the sanctuaries with quieter, higher, weirder views than the coast path.

Fit hikers · park trails from each village, free; check trail status first
