— Stop 04 · Tuscany · WORK BASE

Florence — first classes

Sept 18 – 25 · 7 nights

Warm up the apron. The long Florence week gives Grey his first taste of making it himself — Tuscan pasta and gelato, hands-on.

City center · family cooking class

Fresh pasta + gelato class — Florencetown

Family-friendly · adult + child rates · English · ~3 hrs

Roll and cut fresh Tuscan pasta, then churn gelato to finish — a hands-on class built for families, and the perfect opener for a food-obsessed 12-year-old.

florencetown.com
Historic center · gelato pilgrimage

The Florence gelato crawl

All ages · taste the good stuff before you make it

Before he makes gelato, taste the artisan benchmarks — Florence is packed with them. Learn to read a real gelateria (natural colors, covered tubs) so he knows what "good" tastes like.

florencetown.com · food tours
— Stop 05 · Lazio · WORK BASE

Rome — pasta & pizza

Sept 25 – Oct 2 · 7 nights

A full week in Rome's kitchens — the whole-family pasta class, and a pizza-and-gelato class built for kids.

Trastevere · English-native company

Pasta-making class — Devour Tours

Ages 9 · 12 · 16 · 18 · "primary-school age and up" · ~$96 · 3 hrs

Hands-on fettuccine + ravioli + Roman sauces, ending in a family meal. An English-speaking company built for families — the best whole-group pasta opener.

devourtours.com
Near Piazza Navona · built for kids

Pizza + gelato class for kids — InRome Cooking

Purpose-built for kids · adult accompanies · English

Roll dough, top a pizza in a real oven, then churn gelato and biscotti — a dedicated kids' class, and Grey's pizza fix on the route. Request a family quote for all six.

inromecooking.com
— Stop 06 · Emilia-Romagna · FOOD CAPITAL

Bologna — the food capital

Oct 2 – 4 · 2 nights

The literal food capital of Italy — birthplace of tagliatelle, tortellini, Parmigiano, balsamic, and the modern gelato machine. It's the best hands-on food on the trip, packed into a short weekend, so pick two or three and go deep.

Central Bologna · the classic · sells out

Hands-on pasta — Taste Bologna

Kids 4–10 at their own station (€59) · all ages · English · ~3 hrs

Make tagliatelle, tortellini and tortelloni by hand — the signature skill of the region, and a real keeper. The one to book first; it sells out.

tastebologna.net
Anzola dell'Emilia · gelato HQ

Carpigiani Gelato Museum — family workshop

Designed for kids · only 2 English tours/day (11:30, 14:30) · book ahead

Emilia is where modern gelato is engineered. The Gelato Museum runs hands-on "gelato for families" masterclasses — churn, balance, and taste your own batch. Reserve in advance; English slots are limited.

gelatomuseum.com
Near Modena · the famous morning tour

Parmigiano Reggiano dairy tour

Stroller-friendly sites · adults €22, kids 6–10 €12 · book ahead

Watch the king of cheeses made at dawn, then taste it by age. Mandatory advance booking; pick a kid-friendly dairy on the official portal.

parmigianoreggiano.com · book a dairy
Modena · free · English page

Balsamic acetaia tour — Acetaia di Giorgio

Aging-attic tour + DOP tasting · free for individuals · book by email

See real traditional balsamic age in barrels in an 1860 home, then taste the syrupy DOP stuff (nothing like supermarket balsamic). Free for individual visitors.

acetaiadigiorgio.it
Just outside Bologna · a food playground

Grand Tour Italia — the giant food park

All ages · working farms + factories, rides · park entry free

The huge food destination outside Bologna (the former FICO Eataly World, reopened as Grand Tour Italia) — pizza and gelato made in front of you, working farms and factories, rides. A food kid's dream, if the tight schedule allows. ⚠ Re-confirm it's open ~1 month before the trip.

grandtouritalia.it
Piazza Maggiore · sweet

Chocolate masterclass — Venchi

All ages · make-your-own praline · book on Venchi site

A fun, low-stakes chocolate workshop in the center — tasting plus making your own pralines. Easy treat for a food-first weekend.

venchi.com · workshops
2 nights, so choose Bologna is only 2 nights. Taste Bologna pasta + one of {Carpigiani gelato, a Parmigiano dairy} is a realistic, brilliant weekend. Book the pasta class the moment dates are firm; save Grand Tour Italia for a return trip if the calendar's full.

— Stop 08 · South Tyrol · THE BIRTHDAY

Dolomites — turning 12 on the mountain

Oct 9 – 14 · 5 nights · Grey's 12th is Oct 12

Grey's birthday moves to the Dolomites — and South Tyrol has its own sweet food tradition to make it special. A cable-car day, an alpine lunch, and a birthday dessert made of egg, sugar and mountain air.

Oct 12 · the birthday day

Cable-car day + alpine lunch + Kaiserschmarrn

All ages · gondola up · rifugio lunch · birthday dessert

Ride the Seceda or Alpe di Siusi gondola up into the peaks, lunch at a mountain rifugio, and finish with the birthday classic: Kaiserschmarrn — shredded caramelized pancake with fruit — or warm apple strudel. The full plan is on the celebrations page.

celebrations · Grey's 12th
South Tyrol · autumn harvest tradition

Törggelen — the alpine food afternoon

Family-friendly farm inns · roast chestnuts, new wine, strudel

October is Törggelen season — the local tradition of walking to a farm inn for roast chestnuts, speck, dumplings and sweet fritters. A hands-in-the-food afternoon that's pure South Tyrol, and a perfect birthday-week outing for a food kid.

suedtirol.info · Törggelen
Val Gardena · a mountain dairy

Alpine cheese at a malga

Working mountain hut · taste (and sometimes watch) alpine cheese

Since we have the car this leg, drive up to a malga (alpine dairy hut) for fresh mountain cheese and butter — a different food world from Emilia's Parmigiano, and a great "compare the cheeses" note to end his food education on.

valgardena.it
