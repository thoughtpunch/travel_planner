Milan — the mineral hall

Sept 11 – 13 · 2 nights

The trip opens in Milan. An easy first fix for the rockhound while everyone shakes off the flight.

Giardini Montanelli · cheap

Museo di Storia Naturale — mineralogy & gems

One of Europe's oldest natural-history museums · EU under-18 free · weekday mornings

Milan's civic natural-history museum has a serious mineralogy and gemstone hall — meteorites, crystals, cut stones — plus dinosaurs for the little ones. A gentle, morning-sized first day before the Duomo.

comune.milano.it · Storia Naturale

Carrara, gems & the earth

Cinque Terre Sept 16–18 · Florence Sept 18–25

The rockhounding half of Jude, right on the route. Carrara's marble mountains sit just down the coast from the Cinque Terre, and the Florence week puts him next to Italy's great gem and mineral collections.

Carrara · the marble mountains · near Cinque Terre

Carrara Marble Tour (4×4 quarry)

No minimum · child tiers €25 (6–12) · season ~Mar–Nov

Ride an open-top Land Rover up into the blinding-white quarries Michelangelo sourced from, right into the working cuts. A short hop from the Cinque Terre base (or an easy day off the Florence week). The single best "the earth, up close" day for Jude.

carraramarbletour.it
Pietrasanta · make something

Marble carving workshop

Beginner-friendly · email to confirm a 16-yo · small groups

Hands-on marble sculpture in tiny groups in the sculptors' town next to Carrara — turn the rock obsession into an actual carved piece. Email ahead about Jude's age and a fall date.

carving-stone.com/pietrasanta
Florence · gem in the hand

Le Arti Orafe — 1-day stone-setting

Hands-on · keep your ring · confirm 16-yo by email

Set a real gemstone onto a silver ring and take it home — the most accessible "work with gems" day in Italy, right in the Florence week.

artiorafe.it · craft experience
Florence · La Specola

Mineralogy at La Specola

Medici hardstones · "Fiori della Terra" · €10/€5 · book online

The mineral collection is back on display after ~150 years — Medici crystals and hardstones. Sells out; book ahead. Pairs with the museum's famous anatomical waxes for the whole-family visit.

sma.unifi.it · La Specola
One ground rule Wild fossil and mineral collecting is illegal in Italy — specimens found in the ground are state property. Tell the boys not to pocket finds from trails, beaches, or quarries. The Bletterbach canyon in the Dolomites (below) is a supervised, "look and learn" exception.
— Stop 06 · Emilia-Romagna · MOTOR VALLEY

Bologna — supercar heaven (in 2 nights)

Oct 2 – 4 · 2 nights

Every legendary Italian marque was born within an hour of Bologna, and every one runs a museum or a live factory tour. The catch: this is the trip's tightest stop, so it's realistically one Motor Valley experience, maybe two — not the whole set. Booking shortcut: Maserati, Pagani and custom car days (with transport, since we're carless here) all route through one official operator — Modenatur.

Bologna · in-city · easiest in 2 nights

Ducati Museum + factory tour

No hard minimum · under-18 accompanied · €32 combined

Watch motorcycles built on the line, right in Bologna — no day trip, no car needed. The most realistic pick for a short stop. ⚠ Confirm the factory-line schedule when booking (tours don't run every day).

ducati.com · Borgo Panigale
Maranello + Modena · the big one

Ferrari Museums Pass — both museums, one ticket

Under-19 €12, under-6 free · valid 48 hrs

One pass covers the Museo Ferrari in Maranello and the Enzo Ferrari Museum in Modena — F1 cars, the Hall of Victories. ~18 km apart; a ticket-holder shuttle links them since we won't have a car here.

ferrari.com · museums pass

Factory tour age 6+ (accompanied) · Mon–Fri · no phones on the line

Walk the live V12/V10 production line plus the museum. Sells out — book through the official page well ahead, and arrange a Modenatur transfer.

Maranello · the thrill · ride only

Ride shotgun in a Ferrari / Lambo — Pushstart

Jude rides (no licence needed) · drive needs 21+ & licence

Pro-tutored road drives; their 4-seat Ferraris let Jude ride in back while a licensed adult drives. From ~€120. ⚠ No operator publishes a passenger age floor — WhatsApp to confirm a 16-yo rides before paying.

If cars are the priority, do Ducati here and save the rest for a future trip — or add a day to Bologna.
— Stop 08 · South Tyrol · THE CAR LEG

Dolomites — fossils in the canyon

Oct 9 – 14 · 5 nights · we have a car

The Dolomites are a UNESCO fossil reef lifted three kilometres into the sky — and this is the one leg with a rental car, so the geology finally comes to us on our own schedule.

Aldein/Aldino · the great geo-cut

GEOPARC Bletterbach — the fossil canyon

Guided geology hikes · fossil & mineral visitor centre · all ages

An 8-km, 400-m-deep gorge that slices straight through 40 million years of rock — ammonites, reptile tracks, mineral bands laid bare in the walls. The visitor centre holds the fossil and mineral collections, and guided hikes read the layers for you. The best earth-science day of the whole trip.

bletterbach.info
Ortisei · Val Gardena

Museum Gherdëina — local fossils & minerals

Small alpine museum · English info · rainy-day fix

Val Gardena's town museum has Dolomite fossils, minerals and geology alongside the woodcarving — a perfect weather-backup when the peaks are socked in.

museumgherdeina.it
