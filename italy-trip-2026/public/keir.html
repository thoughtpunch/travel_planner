— Stop 01 · Lombardy

Milan — Leonardo's machines

Sept 11 – 13 · 2 nights

A gentle, hands-on first day to shake off the flight — one of the best science museums in Europe.

The perfect Keir first day

Leonardo da Vinci Science & Technology Museum

Italy's biggest science museum · working models, trains, a real submarine · EU under-18 free

Leonardo's machines built full-size, plus locomotives, planes and the walk-through submarine Toti. Huge, hands-on, and easy to do at a 9-year-old's pace. A brilliant, low-stress way to start the trip.

museoscienza.org
— Stop 02 · Piedmont

Turin — glass elevator & mummies

Sept 13 – 15 · 2 nights

Two easy city days with two big kid-sized wins — a ride straight up the inside of a landmark, and a hall full of mummies.

The Mole · straight up the middle

National Cinema Museum + the glass elevator

All ages · glass lift 85 m up the hollow tower · movie sets & optical tricks

A glass elevator shoots up through the hollow middle of the Mole Antonelliana for the big view, and the Cinema Museum below is all movie sets, giant screens and optical illusions to play with. Pure fun for a 9-year-old.

museocinema.it
Museo Egizio · real mummies

The Egyptian Museum

All ages · mummies & sarcophagi · EU under-18 free

The oldest Egyptian museum in the world — mummies, sarcophagi and 4,000-year-old treasures a nine-year-old can't look away from. Optional bigger day: the mountaintop abbey Sacra di San Michele, by train and shuttle.

museoegizio.it
— Stop 03 · Liguria

Ligurian coast — beach & boats

Sept 15 – 19 · 4 nights · base Chiavari

Four coastal days from our Chiavari base, built for a kid who just wants to swim and ride boats between the painted villages.

Monterosso · the one real beach

Beach day + the coastal ferry

All ages · swim, rock-hop, then hop a boat

Monterosso has the Cinque Terre's proper sandy beach — swim and dig in the morning, then take the coastal ferry past the cliffs to Vernazza and Manarola for a gelato. Water and boats: the perfect one-thing day.

cinqueterre.eu.com · ferry
Between the villages · easy & flat

The coastal train + focaccia

All ages · the little train links all five villages in minutes

When legs are tired, the Cinque Terre train hops between villages in a few minutes each — grab warm focaccia, watch the sea, no big walk required. Keeps the day gentle.

trenitalia.com
— Stop 05 · Lazio · WORK BASE

Rome — gladiator school

Sept 26 – Oct 3 · 7 nights

A full week means Rome can go slow — one big adventure, then plenty of gelato-and-piazza downtime.

Via Appia · everyone's favorite

Gladiator school — Scuola Gladiatori Roma

Minimum age 6 — perfect for Keir · ~€130 · English · ~2 hrs

Tunic on, train with a wooden sword, learn real gladiator moves from a costumed instructor, then a mini-tournament and a certificate. A full morning's adventure and a highlight for a 9-year-old — his big brothers can train alongside him.

gruppostoricoromano.it
Cerveteri · ~45 min · animals

Educational farm — Bambini e Fattoria

9-yo is the target · feed ponies, alpacas, donkeys, goats · €15 kids

Feed and meet the animals, alpaca walks, pony rides — a gentle, hands-on morning built for younger kids, and a soft counterweight to the big-city days. (Sun/holidays.)

bambiniefattoria.it
— Day-trip from Florence · Emilia-Romagna

Bologna — a food playground

Day-trip · 37 min from Florence

An easy day-trip out of the Florence week — and a 9-year-old's idea of heaven is a park made entirely of food.

Just outside Bologna · the perfect Keir day

Grand Tour Italia (the old FICO food park)

All ages · farm animals, rides, pizza & gelato made in front of you · entry free

Farm animals, tractors, pizza and gelato made in front of you, rides and a skating rink — a whole day that feels like play but is really about where food comes from, and it's flat and stroller-easy. ⚠ Re-confirm it's open ~1 month out.

grandtouritalia.it
City center · easy win

Porticoes, gelato & the Two Towers

All ages · covered arcades to run under, a tower to look up at

Bologna's 40 km of porticoes are perfect for a wander in any weather — gelato in hand, crane your neck at the leaning Two Towers, snack through the market. A no-plan, low-key day.

bolognawelcome.com
— Stop 06 · Veneto

Venice · Lido — glass, armour & beach

Oct 3 – 10 · 7 nights

A little watching, a little making, plus a beach island to run on when the day's one big thing is done. (And this is MuMu's city — the reason Venice is on the map.)

Murano · jaw-drop + try it

Watch glassblowing + make a glass bead

Furnace demo all ages to watch · bead-making age 10+ (email re: a 9-yo)

A maestro turns molten glass into a galloping horse in two minutes — mesmerizing. Then a torch-bead class lets you make your own ("no strength, no risk"). It's officially 10+, so email to ask about Keir at nine.

vivovenetia.com
Doge's Palace · knights & weapons

The Doge's Palace armoury

All ages · suits of armor, swords, crossbows · EU under-18 free entry

Skip the paintings and head for the Armeria — real suits of armour, swords and crossbows in the old palace, then across the Bridge of Sighs to the prisons. The sword-and-armour fix that a 9-year-old actually wants.

palazzoducale.visitmuve.it
Right outside the door

Lido beach + vaporetto rides

All ages · free run-around time

The Lido is an actual beach island — free play, and the water-buses themselves are a thrill. The low-key counterweight to a big-activity day.

veneziaunica.it
— Stop 07 · South Tyrol · THE CAR LEG

Dolomites — cable cars & a toboggan

Oct 10 – 14 · 4 nights

The mountains are made for one-big-thing-a-day: ride up, run around a meadow, come down fast. (Grey's birthday is here too — see the celebrations page.)

Val Gardena · up & over

Cable-car up to Seceda / Alpe di Siusi

All ages · gondola up, easy meadow walk, snack at a hut

A gondola lifts you into the jagged Dolomite peaks in minutes — then a flat, easy meadow stroll and a hot chocolate at a mountain hut. All of the wow, none of the climb. The perfect Keir mountain day.

valgardena.it
Alpe di Siusi · downhill fun

Alpine toboggan / summer luge run

Kids ride with an adult · fast, safe, on rails

Ride a wheeled toboggan down a curving track on the mountainside — brakes in your hands, an adult on board for the little ones. The kind of five-minute thrill a 9-year-old asks to do ten times.

seiseralm.it
Keep the pace Keir's rule across every stop: book one anchor activity per day and leave the rest open for boats, gelato, and rest. An overscheduled 9-year-old melts down; a well-paced one is the happiest traveler in the family.

Booking & logistics

All English-friendly, with age rules that include a 9-year-old. Most of Keir's days are ride-up-and-play — funiculars, ferries, beaches, gondolas — so they need little or no advance booking; the exceptions are gladiator school and the Murano bead class (email about a 9-yo). EU under-18s are free at Italian state museums, including the Doge's Palace.
