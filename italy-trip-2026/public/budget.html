— The playbook

How to save money

Ranked by dollars saved. The first four are painless; the last one (cutting a region) is the only way under $35K with both Sicily and Croatia in.

Biggest lever

Monthly lodging rates

Book Bologna (21 nts) and Venice (14 → push to 28) at 28-night monthly rates — Airbnb/Booking knock 30–50% off nightly. Belt towns only.

saves $2,000–3,000

Cook more

4–5 dinners in, not 3

Every home dinner for 6 beats a trattoria by ~$100. Shop Lidl / Eurospin / Conad and the markets (Ballarò, Pignasecca, Quadrilatero, Rialto), not tourist alimentari.

saves $1,500–2,000

Free by law

Under-18s free at state sites

The boys enter the Colosseum, Pompeii, Valley of the Temples, Uffizi, Doge's Palace corridors and every Italian state site free. Pay only for private experiences (Etna, Motor Valley, courses, boats, Dubrovnik walls). Rhys pays adult after his Sept 18 birthday.

saves $500–800

Smarter trains

Regionale + Bimbi Gratis

Use cheap Regionale for day trips (Florence, Modena) over pricey Frecce, and Trenitalia's Bimbi Gratis — kids under 15 ride free with adults, covering Grey (12) and Keir (9).

saves $400–700

Trim the moves

Sea return + fewer van days

Return Split→Ancona by ferry + train instead of the Dubrovnik→Rome flight (−$500); trim Sicily to 5 days for fewer van days (−$600); take the Kotor bus, skip the private driver (−$300).

saves $1,400

The nuclear option

Cut one new region

Dropping either Sicily or Croatia removes ~1 week of living cost plus its ferries/rental — the only lever big enough to reach the original $35K target. Keep both if the money's there; they're the trip's highlights.

saves $3,500–4,500

Two realistic targets ~$38–40K — apply the top four painless levers (monthly rates, cook more, under-18-free, smart trains) and keep every stop. Under $35K — do all of the above and cut one new region. Layer on the card/FX game (Chase Sapphire 3× on dining, Wise EUR for groceries, hedge USD→EUR over June–Aug) for a few hundred more back.

Quick save, city by city

  • Rome — Garbatella apartment, not the center; picnic in Villa Pamphili; the free-entry family rarely needs a Roma Pass.
  • Naples — pizza is genuinely $6–8; Pompeii boys free; Cumana/Metro are pocket change.
  • Sicily — masseria with a kitchen; the van is the cost, so minimize its days.
  • Bologna — your cheapest base: cook, keep grandma's room modest, do Florence on Regionale. Motor Valley museums are the one splurge.
  • Dolomites — short and dear: self-cater, use the Südtirol Guest Pass for free regional transit.
  • Venice — stay in Mestre; a 7-day ACTV vaporetto pass beats singles; groceries on the mainland; picnic, don't eat canal-side.
  • Croatia — already ~40% cheaper: self-cater, eat konoba lunches, take the bus.
