Dolomites — the one car leg

Oct 10–14 (4 nts)The one car legGrey's 12th birthday

The grand finale, and the only stretch where we rent a car — collected in Bolzano, then up into the peaks with a base in Val Gardena / Ortisei. Cable cars to tilted, blade-like ridgelines, Europe's largest alpine meadow, Ladin woodcarving villages, a real 5,300-year-old iceman, and hearty Tyrolean food where German and Italian share every menu. Four focused mountain days — and Grey's 12th birthday lands right in the middle.

— The shortlist

Dolomites Top 10

South Tyrol is bilingual German/Italian and English-friendly, and this is our one leg with a car — so it's built around scenic drives and lifts. EU under-18s are free at Italian state sites.

  1. 1
    Seceda cable car + the knife-edge ridge
    Ortisei → Furnes → Seceda

    Ride the two-stage lift out of Ortisei to the surreal tilted-blade ridgeline at 2,500 m — one of the most photographed skylines on Earth, with almost no climbing needed up top. Confirm the 2026 autumn closing date before you plan a day around it.

    All ages · English · seceda.it
  2. 2
    Alpe di Siusi / Seiser Alm
    Mont Sëuc cable car from Ortisei

    Europe's largest high-alpine meadow, rolling for miles beneath the Sassolungo. Note the car is barred from the plateau during the day — go up on the Mont Sëuc cable car from Ortisei, or drive in only very early or late. Low effort, enormous payoff.

    All ages · English · seiseralm.it
  3. 3
    Val Gardena villages + Ladin woodcarving
    Ortisei · Santa Cristina · Selva

    Our home valley: three storybook villages that speak Ladin, an ancient Alpine language older than Italian or German. Wander the pedestrian centers, watch a woodcarver at work (the valley's centuries-old craft), and soak up the culture between adventures.

    All ages · English · valgardena.it
  4. 4
    Ötzi the Iceman
    South Tyrol Museum of Archaeology, Bolzano

    A real 5,300-year-old glacier mummy with his actual clothes, tools and copper axe, viewed through a frozen window. Eerie, moving, and universally gripping — the ideal bad-weather backup and a natural stop on car pickup/drop-off day.

    All ages · English · reserve · iceman.it
  5. 5
    The great scenic drives
    Gardena & Sella passes · Sellaronda · Passo Pordoi

    This is why we have the car: hairpin up the Gardena Pass and Sella Pass, loop the legendary Sellaronda around the Sella massif, and top out at Passo Pordoi. Jude's dream drive and the whole family's rolling front-row seat to the peaks.

    All ages · car · valgardena.it
  6. 6
    Lago di Braies + Lago di Carezza
    emerald lakes · check late-season access

    Braies — the mirror-still emerald lake with wooden rowboats and a flat lakeside loop — is peak reflection-symmetry beauty; confirm the road/boats haven't closed for the season. Closer to base, tiny Lago di Carezza glows turquoise beneath the Latemar and is an easy roadside stop.

    All ages · prags.bz
  7. 7
    A family hike + a rifugio lunch
    gentle trails off any lift

    Pick one easy meadow trail — Keir-paced, lift-assisted — that ends at a mountain hut for lunch on a sunny terrace: Kaiserschmarrn (shredded caramelized pancake), apple strudel, canederli dumplings. The most Tyrolean afternoon there is.

    All ages · English · suedtirol.info
  8. 8
    Earth Pyramids of Ritten
    above Bolzano · Renon cable car + train

    Clay hoodoos — tall earthen spires each capped by a balancing boulder, pure Escher geology. Ride the Renon cable car from Bolzano, then the little narrow-gauge Renon railway across the plateau to a short, flat walk. Made for Jude the rockhound.

    All ages · trails free · ritten.com
  9. 9
    Castel Roncolo / Schloss Runkelstein
    just outside Bolzano

    The "illustrated castle": the largest secular medieval fresco cycle in the Alps — knights, courtly life, Tristan & Isolde — sprawled across the walls of a crag-top fortress. Illuminated-manuscript energy for Kei, castle-and-knights heaven for the boys. Under-14 free.

    Adults + boys · English · runkelstein.info
  10. 10
    Bletterbach canyon
    Aldein/Aldino · UNESCO geosite

    The "Grand Canyon of South Tyrol": a 400-m gorge slicing through 280-million-year-old rock, with fossils and clear-as-a-textbook geology. Confirm the season hasn't closed, then let Jude the rockhound lead the way down the layers of time.

    Fit families/teens · English · bletterbach.info
— More to do

Deeper cuts

Grown-ups love

A gourmet Ladin dinner

One splurge table: refined Ladin/Alpine cooking in a Val Gardena mountain hotel — think barley, game, fresh trout and local wine, with the peaks going pink outside. A fitting spot for Grey's birthday dinner; book the good room ahead.

valgardena.it

Wine on a sunny hut terrace

Steal an hour on a rifugio terrace with a glass of Lagrein or Gewürztraminer, a speck-and-cheese board, and a wall of dolomite in front of you. The whole reason to slow down up here.

suedtirol.info

The Great War tunnels at Lagazuoi

Ride the Lagazuoi cable car to 2,778 m and walk the WWI front tunnels blasted through the summit — sweeping panorama, sobering history. Confirm the lift is still running in your mid-October window before you drive over.

lagazuoi.it

Family wins

Cable cars, plural

Half the magic here is the ride up. Seceda, Mont Sëuc, Alpe di Siusi, Renon — big glass cabins swinging over pastures to the top with almost no walking. The kids will happily do a lift a day.

valgardena.it

Toboggan runs + playgrounds

Val Gardena's Alpine adventure parks and mountain playgrounds — plus a summer-toboggan run if one's still open — are pure Keir and Grey fuel between the grown-up scenery.

valgardena.it

Watch a woodcarver work

Ortisei has carved wood for 400 years. Duck into a workshop or the local museum to watch a saint or a whole nativity emerge from a block — hands-on craft the kids can actually understand.

museumgherdeina.it
— Weird, wacky & unforgettable

Offbeat & hidden gems

The strange, memorable, off-the-tourist-track finds the boys will actually talk about — vetted, real, and (this being the car leg) all a short-to-medium drive from Val Gardena. Every one confirmed open in our mid-October window.

Jude · ride into the mountain

Ridnaun mine world + the show tunnel

One of the highest mines in Europe, where silver, lead and zinc came out of the rock until 1985. Don a helmet and lamp, walk 200 m into a real ore tunnel, and watch the giant Victorian ore-crushing machines clank back to life. Peak Jude: rocks, engineering, going underground. Open to Oct 31 (Tue–Sun); the full ride-the-train-900 m-in Monteneve tour winds down mid-Oct, so book the Ridnaun site.

All ages · ~1h drive to Ratschings · guided, reserve ahead

bergbaumuseum.it
Rhys + Jude · WWI up close

Cinque Torri open-air war museum

A whole WWI front line — restored trenches, gun emplacements and tunnels you actually climb through — scattered among five toppling rock towers, reached by a short chairlift from Bai de Dones. Free, outdoors, and genuinely gripping for the older boys who want the real thing, not a display case. Lift runs June–October, so mid-Oct just sneaks in.

Boys + adults · ~1h15 drive toward Cortina · free · confirm the lift

5torri.it
Kei + parents · mountain obsession

Messner Mountain Museum Firmian

Reinhold Messner — first man up Everest without oxygen — turned the ruined Sigmundskron castle into a spiralling museum about humanity's relationship with mountains: a labyrinth of towers, staircases and terraces studded with Tibetan art and summit relics. Escher-grade architecture, Voynich-grade obsession. Open October, closed Thursdays.

Adults + kids · ~40 min drive to Bolzano · rain-plan friendly

messner-mountain-museum.it
Parents · eerie + true

The drowned village of Reschensee

A single medieval bell tower stands alone out in a lake — all that shows of Graun, a village flooded in 1950 to build a dam, its houses still on the lakebed. Pure Escher/Voynich unease, and a legend that the bells still ring on winter nights. It's a haul out to the Vinschgau, so make it a full day paired with Merano's gardens below.

All ages · ~2h drive each way · roadside, always open

suedtirol.info
Grey + Kei · botanical + weird

Trauttmansdorff Gardens + Touriseum

Empress Sissi's terraced castle gardens tumble down a hillside in themed worlds — a rainforest greenhouse, a lily pond, cactus deserts — while the Touriseum inside tells 200 years of Alpine tourism through playful automata-style dioramas. Hands-on and colourful for the younger two. Open to Nov 8; note hours shorten and Sundays close from Oct 19.

All ages · ~1h drive to Merano · pair with Reschensee

trauttmansdorff.it
Kei + parents · legends & geology

Museum Ladin Ćiastel de Tor

A castle museum devoted to the Ladins — the tiny ancient culture of our home valleys — and their mythology: the lost Kingdom of Fanes, marmot-princesses and mountain spirits, alongside the geology that built the Dolomites. Half folklore, half rock science: exactly the Voynich-flavoured weirdness the parents love. Open to early Nov, closed Mondays.

All ages · ~35 min drive to Val Badia · indoor rain plan

museumladin.it
Keir · gross-cool · parents · wellness

Get buried in a hay bath

In Fiè/Völs they've wrapped people in warm, damp, gently-fermenting mountain hay since 1903 — you lie packed in it and sweat while the alpine herbs do their thing. Squarely gross-cool for Keir, oddly blissful for the grown-ups, and a story the boys will retell forever. Book a slot at the Heubad hotel spa.

Teens + adults · ~25 min drive to Völs · reserve the spa

hotelheubad.com
Grey · food · this exact season

Törggelen at a mountain farm

South Tyrol's own autumn ritual, and it happens right when we're here: you walk to a farmhouse inn (Buschenschank) for young cloudy wine, roasted chestnuts, speck, dumplings and doughnuts by a wood stove. The most local, most delicious, most only-in-October thing on the whole trip — Grey's belated-birthday feast. Book a farm through the Red Rooster network.

All ages · farms around Bolzano/Isarco · reserve; kids get grape juice

roterhahn.it
