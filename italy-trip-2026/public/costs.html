How this works

Defaults live in costs-data.js; your edits (booked amounts, sliders, budget) save to this browser only. Nothing is uploaded. To make a number the permanent default for everyone, change it in the data file and redeploy. The deeper low/avg/high model is on Budget; the real train fares are on Trains.
