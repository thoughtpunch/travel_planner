— The shortlist

Lake Como Top 10

Ranked for a slow, restorative three nights — ferry-hopping, gardens, and one big activity a day for the 9-year-old. Mornings and early afternoons are family time before Dan's 4–11pm work window.

  1. 1
    Central-lake ferry day — Bellagio, Varenna, Menaggio
    Navigazione Lago di Como · day pass

    The three prettiest towns sit at the lake's crossroads, linked by a constant ferry triangle. Buy a central-lake day pass and hop off wherever looks good — the defining Como day.

    All ages · English · navigazionelaghi.it
  2. 2
    Brunate funicular + Volta lighthouse walk
    from Como town · every 30 min

    A steep century-old funicular climbs from the lakefront to the village of Brunate; from there a short uphill walk reaches the Volta lighthouse for a huge view down the lake. Great legs-and-views combo.

    All ages · English · funicolarecomo.it
  3. 3
    Villa del Balbianello
    Lenno, near Bellagio · reach by boat

    The terraced garden villa from Star Wars Episode II and Casino Royale, on its own wooded point. Cinematic loggias and sculpted greenery — a highlight for Kei and the movie fans alike.

    All ages · English · fondoambiente.it
  4. 4
    Villa Carlotta gardens
    Tremezzo · lakeside

    A botanic paradise of azaleas, camellias, ancient cedars and a bamboo grove wrapped around a lakeside palace. Kei's dream stop; the boys get a museum and grand views.

    All ages · English · villacarlotta.it
  5. 5
    Varenna + Villa Monastero + the Lovers' Walk
    Varenna · east shore

    The lake's most romantic village: a lakeside botanical garden strung with statues, then the "Passeggiata degli Innamorati" clinging to the cliff over the water. Easy, gorgeous, half-day.

    All ages · English · villamonastero.eu
  6. 6
    Como town — Duomo + Tempio Voltiano
    Como lakefront · walkable

    Our base town: a striped Gothic-Renaissance cathedral and, on the waterfront, the Tempio Voltiano honoring Alessandro Volta, who invented the battery here. Electricity history the boys will actually remember.

    All ages · English · visitcomo.eu
  7. 7
    Orrido di Bellano gorge
    Bellano · east shore

    Walkways bolted into a dramatic slot canyon where a river thunders through the rock. Add the spooky "Ca' del Diavolo" (Devil's House) tower — a slice of drama that's very on-brand for this family.

    All ages · English · orridodibellano.com
  8. 8
    An hour on the water — boat or kayak
    Como or Bellagio waterfronts

    Rent a little self-drive boat or a couple of kayaks and get out on the lake yourselves for an hour. Seeing the villas from the water — the way they were meant to be seen — is the memory that sticks.

    All ages · lakefront rentals
  9. 9
    Nesso waterfall village
    Nesso · Como's west shore

    A tiny cascade tumbling between stone houses under an ancient Roman bridge — one of the lake's most photographed hidden corners. Pair with a stop in Menaggio's easygoing lakefront.

    All ages · reachable by boat/bus
  10. 10
    Funicular sunset + lakefront gelato
    Brunate or Como waterfront

    End a day up in Brunate as the light goes gold, or just claim a bench on the Como lungolago with cones in hand. The easy, no-plan win that makes three nights feel like a real rest.

    All ages · free

— Weird, wacky & unforgettable

Offbeat & hidden gems

The strange, memorable, off-the-tourist-track finds the boys will actually talk about — vetted, real, and doable by ferry, funicular, train or bus from Como town.

Kei & Rhys · the cursed island

Isola Comacina & the curse

The lake's only island — sacked in 1169 and cursed by a bishop: "no stone upon stone, no one shall host another here or die a violent death." It's now an open-air ruin of ancient churches you land at by ferry, with an on-brand ghost story Kei will love and enough edge for Rhys.

All ages · short boat from Sala Comacina/Ossuccio · outdoor ruins

comacina.it
Jude · engineering & electricity

Life Electric — Libeskind's spike

A 16.5m twist of steel by architect Daniel Libeskind, planted at the tip of Como's breakwater. Its two opposing sine-waves are literally the voltage between a battery's poles — a monument to Volta that lights up at night. Pure engineering-brain candy for Jude, and a five-minute walk from our base.

All ages · free · walkable on Como lakefront

italia.it
Keir · the vanishing river

Fiumelatte — Italy's shortest river

A milky-white torrent that bursts from a cave in the mountain, runs just 250m, and crashes into the lake — then vanishes completely every mid-October and reappears in March, with no one fully sure why. Leonardo da Vinci climbed up to study it and wrote it into his Codex. We're there in September, so it'll still be roaring.

All ages · train/ferry to Varenna, then a short steep walk up

varennaturismo.com
All of us · lakeshore walk

Greenway del Lago di Como

An 11km footpath on the ancient Roman Via Regina, threading the back lanes of six villages between Colonno and Griante — past hidden gates, mosaics and villa walls the ferries never show you. Marked with brass medallions, flat and easy, and choppable into any length with a bus or boat home.

All ages · bus/ferry to Colonno · do a stage, not the whole thing

greenwaylagodicomo.com
Parents & Keir · Baroque wunderkammer

Sacro Monte di Ossuccio

A cobbled pilgrim path climbs past 14 Baroque chapels stuffed with 230 life-size terracotta figures frozen mid-scene — a spooky, theatrical, UNESCO-listed hillside that's catnip for the Escher/Voynich taste. It looks straight across at Isola Comacina, so you can pair the two.

All ages · near the Greenway above Ossuccio · uphill climb

sacrimonti.org
Rhys & parents · monks & alchemy

Abbazia di Piona — the monks' liqueur

A 12th-century abbey on its own quiet peninsula, still worked by monks under the "pray and work" rule. In their little shop they sell herbal liqueurs, medicinal-herb teas and honey distilled by centuries-old recipe — an alchemist's cabinet you can taste. Rhys can do the first legal tasting; the cloister carvings are the reward for everyone else.

All ages · upper-lake ferry to Colico area · working monastery, dress modestly

comoanditslake.com
Grey & Keir · gross-cool food

Missoltini — the sun-dried lake fish

Como's oldest delicacy: little lake sardines salted, sun-dried for ten days, then pressed under weights in a tin with bay leaves until autumn — a recipe Pliny the Younger already wrote about in Roman times. Grilled and served on toasted polenta, it's the weird-but-tasty dare Grey and Keir will brag about back home.

All ages · lakeside trattorias (best in the villages) · a taste, order the polenta too

tasteatlas.com
Rhys & Jude · dark history

Museo della Fine della Guerra, Dongo

The lakeside town where partisans caught Mussolini in April 1945 as he ran for Switzerland disguised as a German soldier — he was shot the next day just down the shore. An interactive museum inside the actual Palazzo Manzi tells one of Italy's most controversial days with real relics and footage. Heavy, gripping, and honest for the older two.

Best 14+ · upper-lake ferry/bus to Dongo · check open days

museofineguerradongo.it
